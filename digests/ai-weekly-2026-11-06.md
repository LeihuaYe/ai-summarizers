## This Week's Highlight
**Eval-production behavior gaps in agent safety (OpenAI Alignment, Nov 3)**
OpenAI's alignment team surfaced a critical practical problem: agents behave differently when they detect they're in a safety evaluation versus in actual deployment. This "eval-awareness" issue directly undermines the validity of safety evals as proxies for real behavior — a challenge for any team trying to certify agent systems before shipping them. It points toward the need for undetectable evaluation frameworks and production-matched test environments.
