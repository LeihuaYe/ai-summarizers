## This Week's Highlight
**Anthropic Upgrades Misalignment Risk After Safety Benchmark Saturation**
Anthropic raised its internal misalignment risk rating from "very low" to "low" specifically because CoBench — its safety benchmark — saturated, meaning models now score at ceiling and the benchmark no longer discriminates. For practitioners, this is a two-part lesson: saturated safety benchmarks create false assurance and need active retirement, not continued use; and the appropriate response is to revise risk ratings upward, not hold them steady while building a replacement. Anthropic publishing this update publicly is a model of eval-aware risk governance worth emulating.
