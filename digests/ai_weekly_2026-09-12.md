## This Week's Highlight

GPT-6 Astra is the week's most consequential release not because of its benchmark scores but because of what its evaluation triggered: OpenAI's Preparedness Framework rated it "Critical" for autonomous cyber capabilities and restricted its API access accordingly — the first time a public model release has activated a formal capability-gated safety response.
