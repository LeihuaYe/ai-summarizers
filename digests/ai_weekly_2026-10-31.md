## 2. New Benchmarks & Evaluation Frameworks

**Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 (Oct 27–28)**
A major overhaul of the composite AI intelligence ranking, now weighting private tests at 60% of the total score alongside new coding and science tasks. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon leads at score 59 (Claude Opus 5.5 at 58). The shift toward private, harder benchmarks aims to reduce gaming by frontier labs.

## 3. AI Evaluation Research — Notable Papers

**AgentSafeEvo: Adversarial Co-Evolution for LLM Agent Safety** (arXiv:2610.19422, Oct 14)
Proposes a reinforcement-learning attacker-defender co-evolutionary framework for stress-testing LLM agent safety across diverse task types and attack categories. Shows that static safety training can be consistently defeated by adaptive adversaries.
- Source: [arXiv:2610.19422](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19422)

**SentryBench: Cost-Sensitive Benchmark for Adversarial Robustness of Tool-Using LLM Agents in Public-Safety Deployments** (Oct 25)
Introduces a cost-sensitive evaluation framework for tool-using agents deployed in public-safety contexts, measuring how robustness tradeoffs interact with deployment costs.
- Source: [DailyArXiv — Oct 25, 2026](https://github.com/somewordstoolate/DailyArXiv/issues/353)

**CoSA: The Conflict-of-Skills Attack in Real-World LLM Agent Ecosystems** (Oct 26)
Examines a novel attack vector exploiting contradictions between an agent's learned skills, demonstrating exploitable inconsistencies in production agent deployments.
- Source: [DailyArXiv — Oct 26, 2026](https://github.com/zachysun/DailyArXiv/issues/584)

**AgentPhishing: A Benchmark of LLM Email Agents Under Adaptive Attack** (Oct 22)
Benchmark targeting LLM-based email agents under adaptive phishing attacks, measuring how well agents resist social engineering when attackers can observe and adapt to defenses.
- Source: [DailyArXiv — Oct 28, 2026](https://github.com/bddk520/DailyArXiv/issues/423)

**AfterDeleteBench: Auditing Post-Deletion Information Persistence in LLM Agents** (Oct 22)
Investigates whether LLM agents actually "forget" information after deletion requests, finding persistent leakage in multiple tested systems.
- Source: [DailyArXiv — Oct 28, 2026](https://github.com/bddk520/DailyArXiv/issues/423)

**EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence** (Oct 22)
Assesses how agents handle safety-critical decisions when evidence is absent or incomplete, highlighting a gap in standard eval setups that assume full context availability.
- Source: [DailyArXiv — Oct 25, 2026](https://github.com/somewordstoolate/DailyArXiv/issues/353)

## 4. Industry Developments — Safety, Policy & Infrastructure

**Multiple US States Sue OpenAI Over ChatGPT Safety and Minor Protection** (October)
Arizona, Arkansas, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska, West Virginia, and Texas all filed separate state-court actions against OpenAI in October 2026, alleging deceptive safety claims about ChatGPT and violations of laws protecting minors. Forty state AGs separately wrote to demand that any Character.AI acquisition include robust child-safety provisions.

## This Week's Highlight

With Gemini 4 Carbon at #1 and Claude Opus 5.5 one point behind, this methodology change reshuffles rankings compared to public-only leaderboards and signals that the field is trying to make capability measurement harder to manipulate.
