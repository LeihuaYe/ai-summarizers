  > Across 9 prompting strategies, 4 judge models, 27 languages, and over 1M pointwise scores, judge model choice dominates prompting strategy choice, with strategy effects reversing direction across judges and stability degrading in low-resource languages.

- **Reality Check of Inference-Time Context Augmentation** — arXiv | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30983
