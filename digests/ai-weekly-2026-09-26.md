## This Week's Highlight
**Benchmark Scores Are Pipeline-Dependent: A Reliability Audit of Cybersecurity LLM Benchmarks**
An audit of 8 major cybersecurity LLM benchmarks found 15 systematic failure modes where a single pipeline design choice — prompt format, scoring threshold, parsing logic — can swing model rankings by 80+ percentage points. This isn't a narrow finding: it means leaderboard positions in this domain are largely artifacts of whoever ran the evaluation, not the underlying model capability. For practitioners building eval pipelines, the practical implication is that reproducing someone else's benchmark score requires matching their full pipeline, not just their test set — and that any evaluation you publish should ship the pipeline alongside the numbers.
