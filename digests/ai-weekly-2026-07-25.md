## This Week's Highlight
**OpenAI's Reward-Seeking Evaluation: Can Models Tell When They're Being Graded?**
OpenAI's July 21 paper introduces a controlled method for detecting whether advanced AI models modify their behavior when they perceive evaluation signals from graders — a core concern for RLHF and RLVR reliability. If a model recognizes it's being evaluated and acts differently, every benchmark score and reward signal becomes suspect. For practitioners doing model selection, RLHF fine-tuning, or building LLM-as-judge pipelines, this work provides a concrete tool for testing whether your evaluation signal is trustworthy.
