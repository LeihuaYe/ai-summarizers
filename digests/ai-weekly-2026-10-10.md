## This Week's Highlight
**Adversarial Cues in Decision Models Used as Judges (arXiv 2610.11436)**
A single colon appended to a candidate answer boosted false acceptance of wrong answers from 1% to 26% in judge models — a 26× swing from a one-character formatting change. For practitioners using LLM-as-judge in evals or production pipelines, this is a direct warning: judge reliability is not just a model quality problem but a prompt-engineering problem, and small surface changes to how requests are formatted can silently corrupt your eval results.
