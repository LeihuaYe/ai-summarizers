## This Week's Highlight
**STEMBench: Frontier Models Exceeding Postdoc-Level Human Experts (arXiv:2611.24321)**
STEMBench introduces 12 problems calibrated to postdoc difficulty in high-energy physics, astrophysics, and neuroscience — fields where answers are objectively scored by each discipline's established figure of merit. The key finding: at least one frontier model beat the expert-human anchor on 7 of 12 tasks. For practitioners evaluating AI for scientific and research applications, this benchmark is a concrete tool for measuring genuine scientific reasoning versus surface-level pattern matching, and it sets a new bar for what "expert-level" actually means in capability assessment.
