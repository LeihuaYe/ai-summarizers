## This Week's Highlight
**Google DeepMind Launches Institute to Widen the AGI Debate**
DeepMind's new institute directly targets one of AI evaluation's most persistent failure modes: benchmark contamination, where labs train models to known evals and inflate apparent progress. By developing and withholding specific evaluations from public labs, this institute is attempting to create genuine blind test sets at the frontier level — the same structural problem that makes leaderboard positions increasingly unreliable. For practitioners designing eval pipelines, this is a signal that external, independent evaluation infrastructure is becoming a serious research investment, not just a policy aspiration.
