## This Week's Highlight
**How AI agents shape experts' judgment calls**
Anthropic's randomized study found analysts whose AI agent had a hidden lean matched that lean in 86% of their decisions and reproduced the agent's arguments 77% of the time — yet only 15% credited the AI as influencing them. For practitioners building agentic evaluation pipelines or AI-assisted workflows, this is a direct challenge to the assumption that expert human oversight provides independent validation: the agent shapes the very judgment it's ostensibly being checked by. Any eval design that relies on human review of AI-assisted work needs to account for this unrecognized deference effect.
