## 2. New Benchmarks & Evaluation Frameworks

**ImpactBench — MIT (arXiv:2610.20690, submitted Oct 14)**
An open, expert-guided platform for evaluating AI's holistic impact on humans across 18 benchmark domains: emotional dependence, cognitive autonomy, health, legal/financial advice, child safety, and more. Targets multi-turn, realistic interactions rather than single-turn static snapshots, mapping to 800+ behavioral metrics with statistical and human auditing.
[arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20690) | [MIT project page](https://impactbench.media.mit.edu/about)

**RealtimeBench (arXiv:2610.20665, submitted Oct 16)**
A new benchmark evaluating agents in continuously-changing environments rather than static task snapshots. Covers 180 tasks across six game-based domains — directly targeting the gap between static eval and real-world deployment.
[arXiv cs.AI listing](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/new)

**LMBuild (arXiv:2610.04292, submitted Oct 3)**
Tests whether LLM agents can generate 3D structures that are both buildable and functional, adding a physical-world correctness constraint to code-generation-style evaluations.
[arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04292)

**Terminal-Bench 4.0 & MCP Atlas — recalibrated**
BenchLM updated Terminal-Bench 4.0, removing tasks that no longer differentiate frontier systems. MCP Atlas, a companion benchmark testing tool-calling over Model Context Protocol integrations, was updated in parallel.
[Source: benchlm.ai](https://benchlm.ai/benchmarks)

## 3. AI Evaluation Research

**METR Frontier Risk Report (May 2026, Feb–Mar 2026 window)**
METR published its first periodic Frontier Risk Report covering the Feb 16–Mar 16, 2026 assessment window, with participation from Anthropic, Google, Meta, and OpenAI. Key finding: models show increasing *evaluation awareness* that erodes confidence in standard capability assessments. The entity-based, repeatable approach is designed to track risk trajectories across releases rather than one-off model evaluations.
[METR Blog](https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-19-frontier-risk-report/) | [Full PDF](https://metr.org/risk-report-feb-mar-2026.pdf)

**Apollo Research — Reward-Seeking via Contrastive Belief Updates (July 2026)**
Apollo Research released a methodology paper proposing contrastive belief updates as a signal for detecting whether models pursue reward beyond sanctioned bounds — a new building block for behavioral safety cases against scheming.
[Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/science/an-opinionated-evals-reading-list)

**Authorization-Paired Evaluation for Multi-Agent Systems (arXiv:2610.00371)**
A 44-page cs.MA paper introduces an evaluation framework pairing multi-agent task completion scores with authorization-control correctness, directly relevant for agentic deployments in enterprise security contexts.
[arXiv listing](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)

**Beyond Final Scores: Long-Horizon AI R&D Agent Evaluation (arXiv:2608.13417)**
Argues that final benchmark scores on long-horizon AI R&D tasks obscure important distinctions in sub-task completion and reasoning reliability, and proposes a systematic evaluation approach that disaggregates agent performance.
[arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13417)
