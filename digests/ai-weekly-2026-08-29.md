## This Week's Highlight

**Piloting the World's First Double-Blind AI Evaluations (Google DeepMind)**
Benchmark contamination — where models are implicitly or explicitly optimized to score well on known test sets — has become a credibility crisis for AI evaluation. DeepMind's double-blind protocol, which cryptographically isolates evaluators from model identities, is the most structurally rigorous answer to this problem published to date. For practitioners building or procuring AI systems, this methodology sets a new bar: eval results from non-blinded studies should be interpreted with heightened skepticism going forward.
